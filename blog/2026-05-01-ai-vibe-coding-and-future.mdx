There's a lot of talk about Artifial Intelligence (AI) and how it _currently_ is changing the world as we know it.
It is true that AI is shaping a new future for us, but it is also true that AI is not as much of a threat as the car was back in the day, or as the industrial revolution was.

In this blog post, I want to talk about how AI is changing the way we work and live, its impact on our social lives and career choices, and how we can adapt to this new reality.

I feel like it is mandatory to talk about AI through different lenses, and not just through the lens of software development, which is the field I am most familiar with.
To grasp its full impact and to have a more holistic view of the "AI" keyword and what it represents, we need to talk about it through different lenses, such as:

- intelligence, bias, trust, sustainability, accountability
- what is AI, what are LLMs, and how do they work? What are the limitations of AI? Its evolution and its future through model iterations?
- what is vibe coding, and how does it relate to AI?
- the impact of AI in our political landscapes
- the impact of AI in our economic landscapes
- the impact of AI in our education systems
- the impact of AI in our social lives
- the impact of AI in our careers (and especially in software development)
- the impact of AI in our mental health
- the impact of AI in our creativity
- the impact of AI in our future

This blog post is not meant to be an exhaustive analysis of all these topics, but rather an attempt to reflect and have a debate about the future we are heading to, and how we can adapt to it.
